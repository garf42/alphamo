
# Rolex

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Rolex SA founded 1905 by Hans Wilsdorf + Alfred Davis in London as Wilsdorf & Davis; moved to Geneva 1919; renamed Rolex 1908 (Wilsdorf trademark). 1960 Wilsdorf donated entire ownership to Hans Wilsdorf Foundation (private + non-profit) — **uniquely owned by foundation since 1960**. Jean-Frédéric Dufour CEO since 2015 (Patrick Heiniger CEO 1992-2008; Bruno Meier 2008-2014). FY2024 revenue ~CHF 10.6B (~$11.6B, +6.3%) — Rolex remains private but estimates available. 25% global luxury watch market share. Annual production ~1M+ watches. August 25, 2023 **Bucherer acquisition** (undisclosed price) — first major acquisition; Bucherer + Tourneau = 100+ points of sale globally, biggest retail brand in US.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Rolex Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) program launched late 2022** — disrupts traditional secondary market; certified through authorized dealers + Rolex authentication. (2) **Bucherer 2023 acquisition** is architecturally defining — Rolex now operates both manufacturing + retail vertical integration; previously authorized-dealer-only model. (3) **CEO Dufour 2025 warns against viewing luxury watches as investments** — explicit cultural + brand discipline stance against pure-speculation secondary market. (4) Tudor (Rolex sister brand since 1946) certified pre-owned program 2024 + expansion 2025-2026. (5) Hans Wilsdorf Foundation structure means **private + non-profit + Geneva-based + Switzerland-headquartered** + multi-generational architectural discipline since 1960 (~65 years of foundation stewardship).
