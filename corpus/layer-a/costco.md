
# Costco

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Costco founded September 15, 1983 by Jim Sinegal + Jeff Brotman in Seattle. Sinegal had worked for Sol Price at FedMart + Price Club; Brotman attorney from Seattle retailing family. December 1985 NASDAQ IPO at $10/share. 1993 merger with Price Club forming PriceCostco; 1997 rebrand to Costco. Ron Vachris CEO since 2024 (W. Craig Jelinek 2012-2024; Sinegal 1983-2012 founder-CEO). FY2025 net sales $269.9B (+8%); 81M paid memberships; **U.S. renewal rate 92.7%** (industry-leading); membership fee +14% Q1 FY2026 to $1.329B. Kirkland Signature ~$90B sales 2025 (~33% Kirkland penetration). Costco market share 77.7% warehouse club Q1 2025; visits Costco 54.3% / Sam's Club 36.0% / BJ's 9.7%.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **September 2024 membership fee increase** ($60 → $65 individual; $120 → $130 Executive) — first since 2017; tested member elasticity, results favorable. (2) **Kirkland Signature scaling fast** to ~$90B (vs $86B 2024) at 33% penetration + 15%+ margins; CEO Vachris targeting more growth + tariff hedging via Kirkland sourcing relocations. (3) Costco 92.7% US renewal rates + 68.3M individual memberships (+4.6M YoY) vs Sam's Club + BJ's; **structural employee + member loyalty advantage**. (4) **Costco wages**: new $20/hr minimum + $31.90 average + 3-year contract with $1/hr raises 2026 + 2027; 93% employee retention vs industry average ~50%; cultural commitment as architectural feature. (5) Costco grocery share gaining 7.6% → 7.9% → 8.2% (per Walmart entry); Walmart Connect ads + retail media architectural extension into substrate Costco does not pursue (Costco rejects most advertising = architectural differentiation). (6) Sam's Club + BJ's expansion 2025-2026 — Sam's Club adding 15+ clubs annually + Sam's Club pizza delivery competitive response.
