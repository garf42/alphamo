
# Ford Motor Company

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Ford Motor Company founded June 16, 1903 by Henry Ford in Detroit. 1908 Model T introduction; 1913 moving assembly line at Highland Park revolutionized manufacturing (12.5 hours → 93 minutes per Model T). Model T sold 15M+ vehicles by 1927. Jim Farley CEO since October 2020. FY2025 total revenue $187.3B (+1% YoY); FY2026 guidance EBIT $8-10B. Three segments: Ford Blue (gas/hybrid) H1 2025 rev $46.8B at 1.6% EBIT margin; Ford Model e (EV) H1 2025 rev $3.6B at -60.5% EBIT margin; Ford Pro (commercial) H1 2025 rev $18.8B at 12.3% EBIT margin. Market cap ~$60B.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **December 15, 2025 Ford $19.5B writedown** as F-150 Lightning pivots from BEV to EREV (extended-range EV) architecture — major EV strategy reset. (2) Ford Model e 2025 EBIT loss $4.8B → guide $4.0-4.5B 2026 (improvement); CEO admits Model e will keep burning billions for years. (3) Ford Pro (commercial) is the profit engine — $6.84B EBIT 2025 + 12.3% margin offsetting Model e losses + Ford Blue thin margins. (4) **2023 UAW strike resolution costs Ford $8.8B over 4.5-year contract** + 27% general wage increase + $850-900/vehicle labor cost increase; Detroit Three vs Tesla $45/hr + Toyota $55/hr labor cost disadvantage. (5) Chinese BYD + emerging EV pressure not yet major in US/Europe but threatening globally + South America. (6) Stellantis 2025 net loss €22.3B reset reflecting industry-wide EV transition challenges. (7) Ford has been continuously operating 122 years as Ford Motor Company since 1903.
