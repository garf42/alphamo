
# Procter & Gamble

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Procter & Gamble Company founded October 31, 1837 by William Procter + James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio. Modern brand portfolio strategy traces to 1837 Ivory Soap (1879) and 1946 Tide. Listed NYSE since 1891. Jon R. Moeller CEO since November 2021 (David Taylor 2015-2021; A.G. Lafley + Bob McDonald earlier multi-decade-CEO patterns). FY2025 (ending June 2025) revenue $84B (organic +4%); operating margin ~22-23%. ~108K employees globally. Market cap ~$370B. Brand portfolio includes Tide (1946, ~$22B), Pampers ($14B+), Gillette (acquired 2005 $57B), Crest, Olay, Oral-B, Pantene, Head & Shoulders, Bounty, Charmin, Dawn, Always, Vicks, SK-II + dozens more. 21 brands generating $1B+ annual revenue ("billion-dollar brands").

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **GLP-1 substrate shift response** — P&G positioning toward digestive health + nutrient-dense + clean-label + functional products. GLP-1 projected $30-55B annual food/beverage revenue reduction 2030-2034 (21% fewer calories + 31% lower grocery spend). ~25M Americans on GLP-1 by 2030. (2) **2024-2026 strategic recalibration** — Moeller-led portfolio optimization + premiumization + AI integration + supply chain digitization. (3) Brand consolidation trend — P&G focusing on top brands + divesting non-core (Duracell 2014, Pet Care, Beauty Salon brands). (4) Pricing power preserved in FY2025 — organic growth +4% with mixed price + volume. (5) Centenarian consumer goods architecture + multi-decade brand stewardship + decade-scale CPG distribution + retail-relationship architectural discipline.
