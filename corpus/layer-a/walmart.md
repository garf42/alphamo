
# Walmart

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Walmart founded July 2, 1962 by Sam Walton with first Wal-Mart Discount City store in Rogers, Arkansas. First distribution center Bentonville, AR 1971. First Walmart Supercenter Washington, MO 1988. Doug McMillon CEO 2014-2025 transition (John Furner Sam's Club; CEO transition recently). FY2025 (ending January 2025) total revenue $681B. Sam's Club U.S. net sales $90.2B; ex-fuel $79.8B; eCommerce +24%; global eCommerce +16%. Membership income +7.1%. FY2026 revenue trajectory $700B+. Market cap ~$700B (2026). ~2.1M employees globally.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Walmart Connect retail media network exploded**: FY2025 $4.4B global revenue +27% YoY → FY2026 nearly $6.4B (+31%), boosted by Vizio acquisition (announced Feb 2024, closed late 2024). Retail media gross margins >70%. CFO calls ads "got US eCommerce into black"; Q4 FY2026 ads + membership = ~1/3 of operating profit. (2) Walmart 23.6% grocery market share but **declining from 21.1% (2024) to 20.6% (2025)** CPG-measured; Costco gaining (7.6% → 7.9% → 8.2%); Kroger 8.8% → 8.3%. (3) Amazon Ads 79.7% retail media share; Walmart Connect 8.0% (distant 2nd but growing fast). (4) Walmart 37.4% e-grocery market share vs traditional grocery 27.3% combined (Q2 2024) — Walmart dominates e-grocery substitution. (5) Doug McMillon CEO transition occurred recently; CEO succession is structural risk per cross-corpus pattern. (6) Walmart+ membership program continues expanding; Vizio acquisition extends retail media into smart TV advertising substrate.
