
# Goldman Sachs

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Goldman Sachs founded 1869 by Marcus Goldman in NYC; partnership structure 1869-1999, IPO May 4, 1999 ($3.657B raised, 15% float); David Solomon CEO since October 2018. FY2025 results: $58.3B total revenue (+9%), net earnings $17.2B (+20%); investment banking fees $9.34B (+21%); equities trading $4.31B (+25%); fixed income trading $3.11B (+12%); asset & wealth management $16.679B (+2%); wealth management client assets $1.9T. Q1 2026 Global Banking & Markets +19% YoY, AWM +10% YoY. Global M&A 2025: Goldman topped league tables with $1.48T advised (32% market share); JPMorgan $1.1T; Morgan Stanley $1T. Advisory fees: Goldman $4.6B, JPMorgan $3.1B, Morgan Stanley $3B. Overall IB fees: JPMorgan $10.1B vs Goldman $8.9B (JPM ahead via debt + equity capital markets diversification).

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) M&A market 2025 second-busiest year on record — $4.5-5T global volumes (+40% YoY), 68 deals >$10B totaling ~$1.5T, mega-deal renaissance. (2) Marcus consumer banking retreat 2022-2024 — Solomon strategic pivot away from full-scale digital bank after product delays, executive turnover, regulatory missteps, deepening losses; Goldman exited personal loans, scaled back deposit-taking, retains Apple Card partnership but is winding that down. Notable: "we tried to do too much too quickly" — Solomon. (3) AWM rebuilding emphasis — private banking + lending revenues +16% to $3.347B 2025; client assets $1.9T; Solomon's new strategic emphasis. (4) GBM segment dominance — investment banking + trading core franchises performing strongly with 19% Q1 2026 growth; equities trading particularly strong reflecting market making + structured derivatives leadership.

## Prose Synthesis

**Identification:** Goldman Sachs is the elite tier-1 global investment bank + trading + asset management institution founded 1869, partnership 1869-1999, IPO May 1999, with David Solomon CEO since 2018. FY2025: $58.3B revenue (+9%), net earnings $17.2B (+20%), topped 2025 global M&A league tables with $1.48T advised (32% market share); ~$200B market cap. Entry scope is the full Global Banking & Markets + Asset & Wealth Management businesses; excludes the failed Marcus consumer banking initiative scaled back 2022-2024.

**Structural position:** Goldman occupies a multi-position architecture across capital markets, advisory, principal, trading, and asset management within the elite tier-1 bulge bracket — a position supply-restricted by g-SIB capital requirements, regulatory barriers, and 155-year cumulative reputation + relationships.
