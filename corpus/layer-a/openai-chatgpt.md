## Research summary

OpenAI's consumer ChatGPT architecture is the canonical AI-substrate-era consumer
conversational-AI-+-subscription architecture. Launched November 30, 2022 by OpenAI (then
9-year-old research lab founded 2015 by Sam Altman + Elon Musk + Greg Brockman + Ilya
Sutskever + others; transitioned to OpenAI LP capped-profit structure 2019), ChatGPT
reached 100M monthly users January 2023 — fastest consumer product adoption in history at
the time [E]. Subsequent growth: ~180M monthly users by 2024, ~400M by Q1 2025, ~600M+
weekly active users by Q1 2026 [E].

The architecture combines: (a) frontier large-language-models (GPT-4 March 2023, GPT-4
Turbo Nov 2023, GPT-4o May 2024, GPT-4.5 Feb 2025, GPT-5 Aug 2025, additional models +
o1/o3/o5 reasoning models), (b) consumer product-experience (chat interface + voice mode
+ image generation via DALL-E 3 + Sora video + Operator agents + ChatGPT search), (c)
subscription tier structure (Free + Plus $20/mo + Team + Enterprise + Edu + Pro $200/mo),
(d) API platform for developer integrations (separately scoped but architecturally linked)
[E]. ChatGPT FY2025 consumer revenue estimated ~$5-7B with ~$10B+ ARR by mid-2026 [I].

The architecture's critical events include: (a) March 2023 — GPT-4 launch establishing
frontier-capability tier, (b) November 6, 2023 — DevDay + ChatGPT Enterprise + Custom GPTs
launch, (c) **November 17-22, 2023 — Sam Altman fired by board then reinstated after
employee + Microsoft + investor revolt**; defining governance event, (d) May 2024 —
GPT-4o launch with native voice + image + integration, (e) October 2024 — DALL-E 3
integration; Sora video preview, (f) December 2024 — o1 reasoning model + Pro tier $200/mo
launch, (g) January 2025 — Stargate $500B infrastructure-investment announcement (OpenAI
+ SoftBank + Oracle + MGX), (h) August 2025 — GPT-5 launch, (i) ongoing 2025-2026 —
xAI Grok + Anthropic Claude + Google Gemini + Meta Llama competition; Microsoft partnership
restructuring; OpenAI corporate-structure transition toward more-conventional-for-profit
status [E].

OpenAI's corporate structure is uniquely complex: OpenAI Foundation (non-profit) controls
OpenAI LP (capped-profit) which holds operations + Microsoft partnership stake; 2024-2025
restructuring contemplated transitioning to traditional for-profit structure with foundation
retaining substantial-but-non-controlling stake. Microsoft partnership ($13B+ committed)
allows: Microsoft access to OpenAI models for Azure-OpenAI-Service + Copilot integration;
OpenAI access to Azure infrastructure (~50%+ of compute); revenue-share. November 2024 +
ongoing-2025 Microsoft + OpenAI negotiating new terms reflecting OpenAI's $86B+ valuation
[E].

Architecturally, openai-chatgpt is the **canonical AI-substrate-era consumer architecture**
and provides multiple cross-corpus pattern observations:
- **Pattern #3 (Load-bearing-for-civilization-stack) emerging 9th instance**: AI-substrate
  increasingly load-bearing for productivity + knowledge-work + creative-output + code-
  generation + customer-service across multiple industries
- **Pattern #6 (Architectural-discipline-as-asset) instance**: Altman founder-led-CEO-
  continuity + research-organization-discipline; complicated by November 2023 governance
  crisis revealing fragility
- **Capability-asymmetry-dominant architecture** comparable to TSMC + ASML + NVIDIA + early-
  Intel; frontier-model-capability is the asymmetric load-bearing-asset
- **Pattern #1 (Founding-doctrine-as-asset)**: "safe-AGI" + research-doctrine from founding;
  complicated by capped-profit-to-for-profit-transition stress
- **Architecture currently in active expansion** — too early for accumulated-force-stability
  assessment but G-forces emerging at unusual speed (3-year time-to-accumulation in some
  dimensions vs typical decade+)
