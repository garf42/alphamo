
# eBay

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: eBay Inc. founded September 3, 1995 by Pierre Omidyar in California (originally AuctionWeb); IPO September 1998. 2002 PayPal acquired $1.5B. **2015 July 17 PayPal spinoff** (Sub-pattern A from eBay multi-segment perspective per PayPal entry analysis). 2015-onward eBay marketplace + Motors + Collectibles + Luxury + Focus Categories single-architecture entity post-PayPal-spinoff. Jamie Iannone CEO since April 2020 (Devin Wenig 2015-2020). FY2025 Q4 GMV $21.2B (+10%); revenue $3.0B (+15%). Focus Categories +15% YoY (Collectibles + Motors Parts & Accessories + Luxury + Trading Cards). Market cap ~$30B (2026); ~12K employees.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **eBay "enthusiasts" strategic pivot 2023-2026** under Iannone — focus categories (Collectibles + Trading Cards + Motors + Luxury) outpacing rest of marketplace by 11 percentage points; deliberate de-emphasis on general consumer goods + Amazon-substitutability. (2) **Collectors + PSA partnership** for trading card grading + Authenticity Guarantee. (3) **Caramel acquisition** for vehicles (online automotive transaction) — multi-decade Motors extension. (4) **AI substrate extension** — eBay AI tools + listing features + marketing flowing toward focus categories. (5) Post-2015 PayPal spinoff eBay marketplace architecture preserved + de-emphasized general consumer goods + emphasized enthusiast categories — multi-year strategic reorientation discipline. (6) From peak GMV ~$100B+ pre-2015 spinoff (combined) to ~$80B post-spinoff GMV — multi-decade transformation + strategic reorientation + competitive pressure from Amazon + Shopify + Shein + Temu.
