## Research summary

Myspace is the **canonical substrate-shift-defunct social-platform architecture**: the
pre-Facebook social-networking platform that achieved 76M+ peak MAU and $12B-peak-
valuation before being substrate-replaced by Facebook 2008-2011. Founded August 2003 by
Tom Anderson + Chris DeWolfe + Brad Greenspan at eUniverse (later Intermix Media) Los
Angeles. Initial architecture: social-networking + music + customizable-profiles +
HTML-CSS-flexibility + bands-+-musicians-discovery + friends-list + bulletins + comments
[E].

Architecture's defining inflection July 2005: News Corp (Rupert Murdoch) acquired Intermix
Media (owner of Myspace) for $580M. Myspace continued growth under News Corp ownership:
~30M MAU end 2005 → peak ~76M MAU 2008 + peak ~$12B News-Corp-internal-valuation 2008
[E]. 2006-2008: dominant US social-networking platform + ahead of Facebook in MAU + ad-
revenue + cultural-position.

**Facebook substrate-replacement 2008-2011**: April 2008 — Facebook surpassed Myspace in
global MAU. May 2009 — Facebook surpassed Myspace in US MAU. Substrate-replacement
mechanism: (a) Facebook real-name-identity vs Myspace pseudonymous/customizable, (b)
Facebook News Feed algorithmic-content vs Myspace static-profile-customization, (c)
Facebook university-+-real-network seeding-+-graph quality vs Myspace open-anyone, (d)
Facebook mobile-app development ahead of Myspace, (e) Facebook ad-targeting quality
superior, (f) Facebook ecosystem (apps + Like-button + login + integrations) [E].

News Corp wrote-down Myspace substantially + sold to Specific Media June 2011 for $35M
(news-corp-loss-of-$545M+ on $580M acquisition + accumulated-investment-loss). Justin
Timberlake became co-owner + chief-creative-officer at relaunch. Specific Media + Time
Inc + Meredith + dotdash-meredith subsequent owners through 2026 [E].

Architecture's current-state 2026: operating-minimal social-networking + music-discovery
focus + ad-tech-pivot + Time Inc / dotdash-meredith ownership; very low MAU (~7M
estimated; tier-3-or-lower social-networking position); architecture functionally-defunct-
but-brand-IP continues. **Pattern #11 (Failed-architecture-as-cultural-cautionary-asset)
11th instance**: extensive cultural-cautionary-asset accumulation [E].

Architecturally, myspace is canonical:
- **Substrate-shift-defunct social-platform** (Sub-pattern B execution)
- **Pre-Facebook-social-networking architecture-template** that was substrate-replaced by
  Facebook's architecturally-broader-+-graph-quality-+-mobile-+-ecosystem template
- **Pattern #11 11th instance** + saturation continues
- **Cross-corpus parallel to Quotron-Bloomberg**: same pattern of narrower-architectural-
  template substrate-replaced by architecturally-broader template
