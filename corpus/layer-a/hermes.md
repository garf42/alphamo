
# Hermès International

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Hermès founded 1837 by Thierry Hermès as harness workshop in Paris (Rue Basse-du-Rempart). Family-controlled across six generations + Dumas family + multi-generational stewardship; Axel Dumas Executive Chairman since 2014. Birkin bag designed 1984; Kelly bag designed 1956 (renamed 1977). FY2025 revenue €15.2B (~+15% growth — among highest in luxury); Leather Goods + Saddlery ~46% revenue + Birkin/Kelly icon products + multi-decade luxury discipline. Market cap ~$300B (2026); ~22K employees globally. LVMH historical stake controversy 2010-2014 (Arnault 23% stake, settled with €8M fine + agreement to reduce). Hermès remains independent + family-controlled.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Hermès double-digit growth 2023-2025** vs LVMH single-digit decline — single-brand discipline outperforming multi-segment conglomerate during aspirational luxury fatigue. (2) Birkin/Kelly waitlist scrapped years ago; now operates VIP client + wish list + relationship system; 2-quota-bag/customer/year cap + 20-48 hour artisan time per bag. (3) 2025 Birkin/Kelly Sotheby's auction sales +44% YoY; Kelly Mini II selling 282% over retail. (4) 2025 regional pricing +10% on Birkin 25 + Kelly Sellier (vs LVMH <2% increase across portfolio). (5) **$10B → $200B Hermès market cap journey without marketing** — single-brand + craftsmanship + family + multi-generational stewardship discipline. (6) **Industrial-scale craftsmanship constraint** (single artisan per bag + multi-year academy training) is the architectural feature distinguishing Hermès from LVMH/Kering/Richemont scale-driven conglomerates.
