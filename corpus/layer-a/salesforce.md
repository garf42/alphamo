
# Salesforce

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Salesforce founded March 1999 by Marc Benioff + Parker Harris + Dave Moellenhoff + Frank Dominguez; IPO June 2004. Benioff CEO since founding; co-CEO arrangements 2018-2020 (Block) and 2021-2023 (Taylor) both ended. FY2026 (ending January 2026) revenue $41.5B (+10% YoY), RPO $72B (+14%). Major acquisitions: ExactTarget 2013 ($2.5B), Demandware 2016 ($2.8B), MuleSoft 2018 ($6.5B), Tableau 2019 ($15.7B), Slack 2021 ($27.7B), Informatica 2025 ($8B). FY30 revenue target $63B. Market cap ~$280B (2026). 21% CRM market share (12th consecutive year leading) > next 4 competitors combined.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Agentforce AI agent platform launched 2024, hit $540M ARR by Q3 FY26 (+330% YoY)** — 18,500 total deals closed, 9,500 paid; 50% of Fortune 100 using Salesforce AI + Data Cloud. (2) **Data 360 (rebranded Data Cloud) processes 3 trillion tokens/month** — "connective tissue" for AI access to real-time enterprise data; fastest-growing product in Salesforce history. (3) Agentforce + Data 360 combined ARR exceeds $2.9B (+200%+ YoY) including $1.1B Informatica Cloud ARR (acquired 2025) + $800M Agentforce ARR (+169%). (4) Three Agentforce pricing models in 18 months: $125/user add-on (Sales/Service/Field), Flex Credits $0.10/action consumption-based, Agentforce 1 Editions $550/user comprehensive suite — pricing experimentation reflecting AI business model uncertainty. (5) Informatica $8B acquisition closed mid-2025 — extends data integration + governance into unified enterprise AI platform alongside MuleSoft. (6) Microsoft Dynamics 365 +23% Q4 FY25 (5.2% share), HubSpot 20-25% YoY (3-6% share) — Salesforce growth (8-11%) trailing competitors but absolute scale + cumulative position dominant.
