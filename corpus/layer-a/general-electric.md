
# General Electric (Edison-era conglomerate)

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: General Electric founded 1892 via merger of Edison General Electric Company + Thomson-Houston Electric Company supported by Drexel, Morgan & Co.; Thomas Edison resigned from board 1894 + sold all shares. Jack Welch CEO 1981-2001 era of financialization + GE Capital ballooning + aggressive M&A + Six Sigma + "neutron Jack" workforce cuts. Jeff Immelt CEO 2001-2017 era of definancialization attempts + 2008 crisis + 2015 Alstom Power $10B acquisition (disaster). Larry Culp CEO October 2018-2024 as restructuring leader; November 2021 announced 3-way split; January 2023 GE HealthCare spinoff completed; April 2, 2024 GE Vernova + GE Aerospace separation completed. Currently 3 separate publicly traded companies: GE Aerospace (Culp CEO + retained "GE" ticker), GE Vernova (Strazik CEO), GE HealthCare (Arduini CEO).

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **GE conglomerate architecture closed April 2, 2024 via 3-way split** into successor entities. (2) GE Capital ($200B financial assets) progressively wound down 2015-2020 under Immelt + Culp. (3) Culp's 2018-2024 restructuring distinct from Sub-pattern C (NYSE/Lloyd's/Nielsen architecture preserved): GE conglomerate architecture closed, multiple successor entities operate as independent businesses. Maps to **Sub-pattern A (restructured-with-successor-entities) — VOLUNTARY variant vs Standard Oil's FORCIBLE variant**. (4) Three successor entities now operate independently: GE Aerospace ($35-40B revenue, aircraft engines), GE Vernova (~$35B, power generation + renewables + electrification), GE HealthCare ($20B, medical imaging). (5) Aggregate market cap of three successor entities ~$300B+ vs pre-split GE conglomerate often <$100B 2017-2020 — restructuring extracted value via separation despite or because of architecture closure.
