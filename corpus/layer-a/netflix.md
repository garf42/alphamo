
# Netflix

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Netflix founded August 1997 by Reed Hastings + Marc Randolph as DVD-by-mail service in California. Streaming launched January 2007 (decade-scale architectural pivot from DVD-by-mail). 2013 House of Cards original content launch. 2015-2024 global expansion to 190+ countries. Reed Hastings stepped down as CEO January 2023; Greg Peters + Ted Sarandos co-CEOs since (Hastings remains Executive Chairman). FY2025 revenue $40B+ (estimated, +16% YoY); Q2 2025 operating income $3.8B (+45% YoY); operating margin 34% (vs 27% Q2 2024). 325M global subscribers end-2025 (+23M YoY). Ad-tier 94M MAU (May 2025), 55% Q1 2025 sign-ups; password-sharing crackdown 2023-2024 + ad-tier launch November 2022 transformative.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Ad tier breakthrough 2025** — 94M ad-tier MAU + 55% new sign-ups + $30-50 CPM ad pricing (premium positioning + advertiser pushback) + 5.8% Q3 2025 revenue contribution from ads. (2) **Reed Hastings 2023 CEO transition** to Peters + Sarandos co-CEOs — multi-decade founder + cultural + execution discipline transition; long-tenure CEO pattern (Hastings 1997-2023 = 26 years). (3) **Password-sharing crackdown 2023-2024 + ad-tier conversion funnel** — non-paying users converted to ad-tier subscribers without heavy marketing cost. (4) **Operating margin expansion** from 27% Q2 2024 to 34% Q2 2025 — multi-year pricing + ad + scale leverage. (5) **190+ countries + 325M subscribers + content investment ~$17-20B/year** — global streaming infrastructure + content + decade-scale platform. (6) Streaming substrate position — Netflix DVD-by-mail 1997-2007 → streaming 2007-onward — DVD architecture closed + streaming architecture preserved + extended (architectural transformation within continuous Netflix entity).
