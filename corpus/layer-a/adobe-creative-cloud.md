
# Adobe Creative Cloud

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Adobe Inc. founded December 1982 by John Warnock + Charles Geschke; Photoshop launched 1990; Creative Suite era 2003-2013. May 2013 Adobe announced Creative Suite 6 would be the last perpetual-license version — all future products subscription-only via Creative Cloud (CC); transformation completed by 2015. Shantanu Narayen CEO since November 2007. FY2025 results: total ARR entered FY2026 at $25.66B; Creative & Marketing Professional subscription $16.30B (+11% YoY); Business Professionals & Consumers subscription $6.50B (+15%); Digital Experience subscription $1.30B (+11%). FY2026 guide: revenue $25.9-26.1B; $25B share buyback announced. Market cap ~$200B (2026); ~30K employees.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Adobe-Figma $20B acquisition terminated December 2023** after EU + UK + US antitrust pressure; major architectural extension blocked; Figma remains independent + IPO January 2025. (2) **Adobe Firefly + Agentic AI extension** — Firefly evolved into agentic creative engine with Firefly AI Assistant 2026; commercial-safe training on Adobe Stock + public domain + licensed content as architectural feature distinguishing from generative AI competitors. (3) **OpenAI Sora + Kling AI + Canva Magic Studio + Figma AI + Microsoft Designer + Leonardo.ai aggressive competitive substitution** — generative AI substrate fundamentally challenging Adobe's creative software architecture; freemium + free alternatives (Microsoft Designer + free models) pressuring CC subscription model. (4) Reporting changes FY2026: Adobe shifted to customer-group subscription revenue (Creative & Marketing Pros vs Business Pros & Consumers) reporting structure — strategic positioning move. (5) 2013 Creative Cloud subscription transformation was canonical perpetual-license-to-SaaS architectural pivot; piracy ~10:1 ratio + "melting ice cube" perpetual revenue + 50K+ Change.org backlash + over 1M subscribers by end-2013 + full transition by 2015 — successful Sub-pattern C-adjacent transformation (architecture preserved + business model fundamentally changed).
