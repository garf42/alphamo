
# Ferrari

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Ferrari S.p.A. founded 1947 by Enzo Ferrari in Maranello, Italy (Auto Avio Costruzioni 1939 racing-only predecessor). Fiat (later FCA, now Stellantis) acquired 50% 1969 + grew to 90%; 2016 IPO + spin-off from Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA). Benedetto Vigna CEO since September 2021. FY2025 revenue €7.1B (+~6% YoY); FY2030 target €9.0B (conservative 15-20K annual unit growth). Production deliberately constrained at ~13.7K cars 2024 + ~14K 2025; ICE 40% / hybrid 40% / EV 20% by 2030 (EV target halved from 40% original). Market cap ~$80B (2026); ~5K employees. Exor (Agnelli family holding) major shareholder (~24%); Pietro Ferrari (Enzo's grandson) ~10%.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Ferrari 2030 EV target halved to 20%** (from 40%) — Vigna explicitly slowing EV transition + extending ICE + hybrid + scarcity-engineering strategy. (2) **Purosangue SUV** launched 2022 deliberately limited to <20% of unit volume + scarcity discipline. (3) **F1 success 2024-2025** (Charles Leclerc + Carlos Sainz + Lewis Hamilton 2025 transfer from Mercedes; Hamilton 7-time champion + Ferrari arrival huge cultural event); F1 = identity + cultural asset architectural extension. (4) **"Luxury's Law of Constraint"** explicit Ferrari strategy — Vigna emphasizes scarcity engineering + multi-decade luxury + ICE + hybrid + cultural + Maranello + Italian craftsmanship discipline. (5) Ferrari ~30% operating margin (among highest auto manufacturers) via scarcity + premium pricing. (6) **Exor + Agnelli family multi-decade stewardship** alongside Pietro Ferrari + Italian + multi-generational + scarcity engineering + Maranello + craftsmanship discipline.
