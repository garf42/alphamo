
# IQVIA (formerly IMS Health)

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: IMS Health traces to Intercontinental Medical Statistics 1954 (founder Bill Frohlich + Arthur Sackler); first syndicated study 1957 (West German pharmaceutical market sales audit). 2010 taken private by TPG + CPPIB + Leonard Green for $5.2B. 2014 IPO as IMS Health Holdings. October 3, 2016 "merger of equals" with Quintiles Transnational Holdings ($8.8B deal, $7.2B combined pro forma 2015 revenue); IMS Health merged into Quintiles with Quintiles as surviving entity renamed Quintiles IMS Holdings. November 6, 2017 renamed IQVIA (I=IMS + Q=Quintiles + VIA). FY2025 TTM revenue $16.3B; ~88K employees globally; ~$40B market cap. Three reportable segments: Technology & Analytics Solutions (TAS) $1.821B Q4 2025 (+9.8%), Research & Development Solutions (R&DS) $2.333B Q4 2025 (+9.9%) with $32.7B backlog at year-end, Contract Sales & Medical Solutions (CSMS) $210M Q4 2025 (+18.6%).

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) March 2026 IQVIA.ai launch — unified agentic AI platform co-developed with NVIDIA leveraging IQVIA's healthcare data + specialized AI agents for clinical development + commercialization + real-world evidence; direct response to Veeva AI (April 2025). (2) Life sciences CRM competitive landscape — Veeva Vault CRM holds ~80% global market share + 9 of top 20 biopharma; IQVIA OCE supporting customers through 2029 with ~400 customers; Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud launched Oct 2025 with 70+ customers. Pricing asymmetry: Veeva ~$10-15K/year per certification, IQVIA OCE ~$3K — IQVIA serving small/mid-pharma cost-conscious segment. (3) R&DS backlog $32.7B at end-2025 with ~$8.3B expected to convert within 12 months — strong forward visibility. (4) The 2016 IMS Health + Quintiles merger was architecturally analogous to S&P + IHS Markit 2022 — merger-of-equals creating multi-segment information+services architecture rather than restructuring-of-failing-architecture. Pre-merger IMS Health was healthcare data + analytics; pre-merger Quintiles was contract research organization; combined IQVIA spans both flows. (5) IMS Health 2010 PE buyout + 2014 IPO + 2016 Quintiles merger sequence creates 3-restructuring history within ~6 years.

**Identification:** IQVIA Holdings is the healthcare data + analytics + clinical research + CRO multi-segment architecture, traces to Intercontinental Medical Statistics 1954, currently in form post-October 2016 merger-of-equals between IMS Health and Quintiles ($8.8B deal); renamed IQVIA November 2017. FY2025 TTM revenue $16.3B, ~$40B market cap, ~88K employees. Three reportable segments: Technology & Analytics Solutions (TAS), Research & Development Solutions (R&DS), Contract Sales & Medical Solutions (CSMS) with $32.7B R&DS backlog at end-2025.

**Structural position:** IQVIA occupies multi-segment healthcare-data + analytics + CRO + services architecture spanning 71 years of cumulative pharma data asset (G3) plus 44 years of Quintiles CRO heritage (G5).
