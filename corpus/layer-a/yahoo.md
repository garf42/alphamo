## Research summary

Yahoo was the canonical late-1990s/early-2000s internet-portal + search + advertising-
supported-content architecture. Jerry Yang + David Filo founded "Jerry and David's Guide
to the World Wide Web" 1994 Stanford → renamed Yahoo 1995. April 12, 1996 — IPO at $13/
share ($1.1B valuation). January 2000 — peak market cap ~$125B [E]. Yahoo's architecture
combined: web-directory + search + Yahoo Mail + Yahoo Finance + Yahoo News + Yahoo Sports
+ Yahoo Messenger + Flickr + Yahoo Maps + GeoCities + various-acquired-properties + ad-
serving + Yahoo Search Marketing — multi-vertical-internet-portal architecture supported
by display + search advertising [E].

The architecture's defining defeat: Google search (founded 1998) progressively overtook
Yahoo Search 2000-2007. Yahoo's strategic-failures included: (a) 1998 — Yahoo declined to
acquire Google (Larry Page + Sergey Brin offered to sell Google for $1M; Yahoo declined),
(b) 2002 — Yahoo paid $1.6B to acquire Overture (paid-search pioneer) but Google AdWords
+ AdSense had already developed superior architecture, (c) 2006 — Yahoo licensed Google
search results temporarily, (d) 2007 — Panama search-advertising overhaul launched late;
Google had ~70% search share + Yahoo ~20%, (e) 2008 — Microsoft offered $44.6B unsolicited
acquisition; Yahoo board (Jerry Yang) rejected → Microsoft withdrew, (f) 2009 — Carol Bartz
CEO; abandoned search advertising; outsourced to Microsoft Bing under 10-year deal, (g)
2010-2012 — Bartz + Scott Thompson + Tim Morse CEO instability, (h) July 2012 — Marissa
Mayer CEO from Google; turnaround attempt 2012-2017 [E].

Mayer-era strategies included: Tumblr acquisition $1.1B 2013, mobile-first investment +
Flurry analytics + BrightRoll video ads + Polyvore + numerous-acqui-hires, focus on
"mobile + social + video + native" advertising. 2016 — Verizon agreed to acquire Yahoo's
internet-business $4.83B (later reduced to $4.48B after Yahoo disclosed two massive data-
breaches: 500M accounts 2014 + 3B accounts 2013). June 13, 2017 — sale closed. Yahoo
became part of Verizon Media Group (later Verizon Media). Residual Yahoo became Altaba
holding company (primarily Alibaba 15% + Yahoo Japan 35% stakes; Altaba dissolved 2019)
[E].

2021 — Verizon sold Verizon Media (Yahoo + AOL) to Apollo Global Management $5B. Renamed
Yahoo Inc under Jim Lanzone CEO (formerly Tinder CEO + CBS Interactive CEO). Architecture
operates as: Yahoo Mail + Yahoo Finance + Yahoo Sports + Yahoo News + AOL Mail at
substantially-reduced scale + niche-position in financial-data + sports + news content +
advertising-platform [E]. Yahoo Finance retains tier-1 financial-news position; Yahoo
Sports + Yahoo Mail maintain niche user bases; broader portal-+-search architecture defunct.

Architecturally, yahoo is **canonical Sub-pattern B substrate-shift case** per watchpoint
#1 with sub-variant: **multi-acquisition-substrate-shift-during-extended-decline**. Unlike
Polaroid + Kodak (single-substrate-shift) + Blockbuster (single-substrate-shift), Yahoo
experienced multiple-substrate-shifts over 25 years: (a) Google search-architecture
substrate-attack 1998-2007, (b) mobile substrate-shift 2007-2012, (c) social-network
substrate-shift 2007-2012, (d) Facebook + Instagram + later-platforms attention-capture
shift, (e) Amazon ad-tier-1-emergence 2017-onward. Architecture defunct as portal-+-search
+ tier-1-internet-property; niche-persistence via Yahoo Finance + Yahoo Sports + Yahoo
Mail under Apollo ownership at ~3-5% of peak market cap scale.

Also: **failed-architecture-as-cultural-cautionary-asset (4th instance)** — Yahoo's $1M
Google decline 1998 + $44.6B Microsoft offer rejection 2008 + multi-CEO + Mayer-era
acquisitions are extensively-cited in business-school + tech-strategy literature as
canonical missed-opportunities + slow-decline anti-pattern.
