
# Boeing (Commercial Aircraft Architecture in Boeing-Airbus Duopoly)

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Boeing founded 1916 by William Boeing (then Pacific Aero Products Co., renamed Boeing Airplane Company 1917). 1997 McDonnell Douglas merger created modern Boeing. Kelly Ortberg CEO since August 2024 (Dave Calhoun resigned). FY2025 revenue $89.5B (+34% YoY recovery from 2024 trough). FY2026 free cash flow guide $1-3B. Boeing 737 production 38/month Q3 2025, jointly agreed with FAA → 42/month, targeting 47 in 2026, 52-57 later. 787 at 8/month Q1 2026 targeting 10/month with $1B site expansion (Nov 2025 groundbreaking). 2025 narrowbody orders: Airbus 504 A320neo + 40 A220 = 544 vs Boeing 461 737 MAX; widebody: Boeing 59% backlog vs Airbus 41%.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Boeing in deep architectural crisis since 2018 MAX crashes (Lion Air Oct 2018, Ethiopian Mar 2019 — 346 dead)** + Jan 5, 2024 Alaska Airlines door plug blowout + July 8, 2024 criminal fraud guilty plea + Dec 5, 2024 plea agreement REJECTED by judge. (2) **Ortberg restructuring** — 17,000 layoffs (~10% global workforce); 777X program delays; 32,000+ machinist strike resolved Q4 2024 with 35% pay hike over 4 years; trust + cultural + execution discipline systemic rebuild. (3) Narrowbody market share: Airbus 54% vs Boeing 35% (Airbus established lead post-MAX crashes); narrowbody = ~78.69% of 2025 deliveries. (4) Widebody: Boeing 59% backlog dominance retained — 787 + 777X + 767 freighter. (5) **COMAC C919 (China) emerging** with ~10% narrowbody backlog share; first credible duopoly disruptor since 1970s. (6) FY2025 +34% revenue recovery from 2024 trough but trust + culture + execution remains existential challenge. (7) Boeing fits Sub-pattern C-adjacent (restructured-but-preserved via cultural + execution discipline rebuild, not corporate restructuring).
