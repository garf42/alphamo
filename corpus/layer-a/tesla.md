
# Tesla

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Tesla incorporated July 1, 2003 by Martin Eberhard + Marc Tarpenning; Elon Musk joined 2004 + key strategic leader since. Roadster 2008; Model S 2012; Model X 2015; Model 3 2017; Model Y 2020; Cybertruck 2023. FY2025 deliveries 1,636,129 vehicles (-8.6% YoY from 1,789,226 in 2024); $4.4B GAAP operating income + $3.8B GAAP net income; $5.9B non-GAAP net income. Energy deployments 14.2 GWh record. Q1 2026 deliveries 358,023 (Model 3/Y 341,893 + 16,130 other). **BYD overtook Tesla as world's largest EV seller in 2025** — BYD 2.26M EVs vs Tesla 1.64M (BYD +28% YoY).

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **June 2025 Tesla Robotaxi service launched Austin** (limited supervised pilot) → January 2026 expanded with unsupervised vehicles → April 2026 Dallas + Houston expansion. (2) **Cybercab production began Q2 2026** at Gigafactory Texas; <$30K target price; production-primed 2-passenger autonomous-only design (no steering wheel/pedals). (3) **Optimus humanoid robot** progressing — Tesla announced production-primed design with $20-30K target sale price; March 2026 Austin Autonomy Pop-Up event showcased Cybercab + Optimus + FSD live. (4) **Musk political forays** + end of US EV tax credits negatively affected Tesla 2025 demand particularly Europe. (5) **BYD 12.1% global BEV share vs Tesla 8.8%** — Tesla overtaken in volume but Tesla remains tier-1 in revenue + brand + autonomy + premium segments. (6) Energy storage 14.2 GWh deployments record; multi-segment architecture extending beyond vehicles into energy + AI + robotics.
