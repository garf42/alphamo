## Research summary

Nokia Mobile Phones was the canonical pre-iPhone-era global-mobile-handset manufacturer
architecture: Nordic-engineering-+-manufacturing-+-distribution-scale integrated mobile-
phone-hardware-+-software business that peaked at ~40% global handset market share +
~$56B revenue 2007 [E]. Nokia Corporation traces to 1865 paper/rubber/cable conglomerate;
exited multiple industries 1980s-1990s to focus on mobile-phones + telecom-networks.
Mobile Phones division established 1992; Jorma Ollila CEO 1992-2006 led the architectural
build-out [E].

Architecture peaked 2007-2008: ~40% global handset share + ~$56B Mobile Phones revenue +
the iconic 6310i + 3310 + Communicator + N-Series + later N95 phones. Nokia phones were
tier-1 in every region globally — uncommon-true-globalization for consumer-electronics
brand [E]. Symbian OS (Nokia + Sony Ericsson + Motorola + Samsung joint-venture) was the
default smartphone-OS 2000-2010. Categories: feature-phones (huge volume; mass-market-
global) + smartphones (Symbian-based + later Windows Phone) + business + designer (Vertu
luxury-phones subsidiary 2002-2012, sold).

The substrate-shift defeat 2007-2013: Apple iPhone June 2007 + Android October 2008 + iOS
App Store March 2008 + Google Play October 2008 redefined smartphone architecture
fundamentally. Nokia's responses failed: (a) Symbian OS inadequate-for-multi-touch-+-apps
ecosystem competition, (b) 2010 — Stephen Elop (from Microsoft) became Nokia CEO + famous
"burning platform memo" February 2011 declaring Symbian uncompetitive, (c) February 2011
— Microsoft-Nokia partnership announced: Nokia phones would switch to Windows Phone OS,
abandoning Symbian + MeeGo, (d) 2012-2013 — Lumia smartphones launched on Windows Phone;
sales disappointing + share decline accelerating, (e) September 2013 — Microsoft announced
$7.2B acquisition of Nokia's Devices & Services business [E].

April 25, 2014 — Microsoft closed Nokia Devices & Services acquisition $7.17B. Microsoft
Mobile operated 2014-2016 selling Lumia phones; 7,800 layoffs July 2015 + $7.6B writedown
+ Microsoft effectively wrote off entire Nokia acquisition. May 2016 — Microsoft sold
remaining feature-phone business to HMD Global (Finnish startup) + manufacturing operations
to Foxconn subsidiary FIH Mobile $350M total. Microsoft exited Lumia / Windows Phone
business [E].

May 2016-present — HMD Global operates Nokia-branded phones via 10-year brand-licensee
agreement. HMD launched Nokia 3 + 5 + 6 + 8 in 2017 on Android; reintroduced Nokia 3310
(2017); Nokia 9 PureView (2019); later mid-range + entry-level Android phones globally.
HMD's volume substantially less than Nokia Mobile Phones peak; Nokia-branded-phones-on-
Android are tier-3 globally [E]. May 2024 — HMD Global announced strategic shift: launching
HMD-branded phones in addition to Nokia-branded; relationship with Nokia Corporation
continuing but evolving. Architecture continues as niche-persistence-via-third-party-
licensee [E].

Architecturally, nokia-phones is the **canonical Sub-pattern B with multi-stage successor-
mechanism** alongside Polaroid + Kodak + Blockbuster + Sears + Yahoo (Sub-pattern B
substrate-shift-defunct-with-niche-persistence cases). **Multi-stage successor mechanism**
distinguishes Nokia from other Sub-pattern B cases: (a) Nokia-corporate-vehicle pivoted
to networks-+-licensing (Sub-pattern D voluntary-transformation for corporate-vehicle), (b)
phone-architecture went to Microsoft 2014-2016 (Microsoft Mobile interlude), (c) brand-
licensee HMD Global continues phone-architecture-with-Nokia-branding 2016-present at
substantially-reduced scale. Three-successor-entity-sequence (Microsoft → HMD → potentially-
HMD-+-Nokia-corporate-vehicle-jointly).
