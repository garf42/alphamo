## Research summary

MoviePass is the **canonical recent-era business-model-fragility architecture**: subscription-
pricing-below-COGS + adverse-selection + cash-burn + investor-pressure + collapse cycle
executed at high-visibility-scale 2017-2019. Originally founded 2011 by Stacy Spikes +
Hamet Watt as movie-theater-subscription-service; initial pricing tiers ranged ~$35-50/
month for limited movies; modest-customer-base ~20K subscribers 2011-2016 with sustainable-
unit-economics-at-that-pricing [E].

**Architectural inflection August 2017**: Helios + Matheson Analytics (HMNY, public-
company-stock-promotion focus + former-IT-services) acquired controlling-stake in
MoviePass + installed Mitch Lowe (former Netflix + Redbox executive) as CEO + slashed
subscription-pricing to **$9.95/month unlimited movies** (one movie per day at any theater)
[E]. Architecture-pricing-decision was below-COGS: average movie-ticket price ~$9-12 in
2017; MoviePass paid theaters full-ticket-price; subscribers attending 2+ movies/month
generated structural-loss-per-subscriber. Pricing-driven-customer-acquisition-surge: ~20K
subscribers August 2017 → 600K October 2017 → 1M+ January 2018 → ~3M peak 2018 [E].

**Cash-burn cycle 2017-2018**: ~$45M operating loss Q4 2017 + ~$150M+ cumulative loss
2017-2018 + ~$300M+ cumulative loss through collapse. Helios + Matheson stock-price-as-
funding-mechanism: HMNY raised capital via public-equity-offerings + dilutive-stock-
issuances + ATM-offerings + reverse-stock-splits + ~$300M+ raised through 2018-2019 to
fund MoviePass operating losses [E]. MoviePass introduced restrictions + surge-pricing +
peak-pricing + new-customer-restrictions + customer-service-disruptions through 2018-2019
attempting to slow cash-burn; customer-experience-degradation accelerated churn [E].

**Collapse 2019**: July 2019 service-suspensions + September 2019 service-shutdown +
HMNY delisted from NASDAQ; January 2020 Helios + Matheson Chapter 7 bankruptcy. ~$300M+
investor-capital lost; ~3M subscribers stranded; SEC investigation + DOJ-class-action +
HMNY-executive (Theodore Farnsworth + Mitch Lowe) fraud charges 2022 for misleading-
investors about subscription-economics + customer-data-claims [E]. Farnsworth + Lowe
indicted September 2022 + Lowe convicted by jury October 2024 (sentencing pending) +
Farnsworth pleaded guilty August 2023 + sentenced + cooperated.

**Spikes-relaunched 2022**: Stacy Spikes reacquired MoviePass-brand-IP-from-bankruptcy
2021 + relaunched MoviePass 2022 with credit-based-system + sustainable-pricing (~$10-30/
month tiered-credit-based) + lessons-learned. Modest-customer-base + sustainable
architecture distinct from 2017-2019 architecture [E].

Architecturally, moviepass is canonical:
- **Subscription-pricing-below-COGS architectural-failure-template**: scale-customer-
  acquisition via below-cost-pricing + investor-fund-burn + collapse
- **Adverse-selection-by-design failure**: power-users (movie-attendance-2+/month)
  generated structural-loss; design flaw not adverse-selection-emerged-organically
- **Pattern #11 (Failed-architecture-as-cultural-cautionary-asset) 10th instance**:
  MoviePass + Mitch-Lowe-conviction + canonical recent-era business-model-fragility case-
  study + financial-press recurring-reference
- **Operator-fraud-charges added 2022 (Farnsworth + Lowe)** — partial fraud-as-architecture
  overlap with theranos; investigation revealed misleading-investor-claims + customer-
  data misrepresentations
