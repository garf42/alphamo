
# Amazon Retail

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Amazon founded July 5, 1994 by Jeff Bezos in Seattle garage (originally "Cadabra"); Bezos CEO 1994-2021; Andy Jassy CEO since July 2021. Online bookseller → Marketplace (3P sellers) → Prime (2005) → Kindle (2007) → AWS (2006 — separate entry) → Whole Foods (2017, $13.7B) → Buy with Prime + omnichannel. FY2025 net sales $716.9B (+12% from $638B 2024); Amazon Prime 240M+ members globally mid-2026; 13B+ items delivered same/next-day globally 2025 (+8B US same/next-day +30%). Market cap ~$1.8T (Amazon overall). Third-party sellers ~1.9M active globally (~1.1M US); 3P sellers >60% of unit sales; 30K FBA sellers >$1M/year + 200K >$100K/year 2026.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Amazon Ads $68B+ FY2025** (vs Walmart Connect $4.4B) — Amazon Ads Q1 2025 $13.9B (+18%), Q2 2025 $15.69B (+23%); 9.36% of Amazon revenue. WARC projects $67-79B 2025-2026. **Amazon retail media 79.7% US share vs Walmart Connect 8.0% distant 2nd.** (2) Same/next-day delivery dominance via regionalized fulfillment network (~190 fulfillment centers + last-mile). (3) Whole Foods 2017 acquisition + grocery substrate extension; Amazon Prime + Whole Foods + Amazon Fresh + Whole Foods + Buy with Prime + omnichannel grocery + delivery. (4) 40.5% US online retail market share Q4 FY2025 vs Walmart 9.2% + Apple 3.2%. (5) Amazon ad business higher operating margin than retail or logistics — key contributor to profitability per earnings call. (6) **Bezos CEO 1994-2021 → Jassy 2021-present**: 27-year founder-CEO tenure (one of longest in corpus, parallel to Huang NVIDIA 33 years).
