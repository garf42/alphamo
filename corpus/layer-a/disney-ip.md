
# Disney IP Library

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Walt Disney Company founded 1923 by Walt and Roy Disney. Major IP-architecture-defining acquisitions: Pixar 2006 ($7.4B), Marvel 2009 (~$4B), Lucasfilm 2012 ($4.05B), 21st Century Fox 2019 ($71.3B — second largest media acquisition in history). Marvel has generated $30B+ box office since acquisition + $13.2B in cumulative value; Star Wars $12B+ in cumulative value since acquisition. FY2025 streaming operating income $1.3B (vs Disney+ launch 2019 → $11B+ cumulative losses through 2023); fiscal 2026 guidance 10% operating margins for Disney+ + Hulu combined. Bob Iger CEO since November 2022 return (after Bob Chapek's brief tenure 2020-2022); ~225K employees globally; ~$200B market cap.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) Streaming profitability turnaround — Q2 2025 streaming operating loss down to $18M from $659M YoY; $1B+ operating earnings guidance fiscal 2025 then $1.3B actual; Iger restructuring + 7,000 layoffs over 2 years + ~$1B DTC operating income improvement in 3 quarters. (2) Disney's 2026 IP power play — Avengers: Doomsday with Robert Downey Jr. + Russo Brothers anticipated; Pixar's Hoppers; three-year MCU + Star Wars + Pixar pipeline. (3) Early 2025 Epic Games $1.5B equity investment — Disney building persistent universes for IP in gaming; signals pivot toward immersive digital experiences. (4) Paramount Skydance won bidding war for Warner Bros. Discovery February 2026 (~$81B, $111B with debt); Netflix withdrew December 2025; competitive landscape consolidating — Paramount-WBD merger creates combined company with Game of Thrones + Mission Impossible + Harry Potter + Top Gun + DC + SpongeBob IP. Disney now faces a more concentrated competitive set: Netflix (highest profitability +82% growth to $5.28B), Paramount-WBD (post-merger), Amazon, Apple. (5) Streaming losses cumulative since 2019 launch >$11B — sets up Disney+ as the most expensive bet in Disney history; profitability now achieved validates the architectural extension into streaming.
