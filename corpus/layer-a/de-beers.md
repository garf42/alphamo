
# De Beers (cartel era)

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: De Beers Consolidated Mines founded 1888 by Cecil Rhodes consolidating multiple diamond mines in Kimberley, South Africa. Ernest Oppenheimer became chairman 1929 and family controlled until 2012; Anglo American acquired remaining Oppenheimer family 40% stake 2012 for $5.1B (now 85% Anglo + 15% Botswana). Cartel architecture peak 1888-2000s — Central Selling Organisation (CSO, founded 1934) controlled ~90% world diamond supply via single-channel rough diamond marketing. "A diamond is forever" Frances Gerety/N.W. Ayer 1947 marketing campaign created manufactured demand. 2000-2001 EU + US antitrust pressure + Russia/Alrosa + Australia/Argyle entry ended cartel; De Beers shifted from supply-control to brand-marketing approach. Current 2025: De Beers underlying EBITDA loss $511M 2025 (from $25M 2024); $2.3B impairment 2024 + $2.9B writedown + $1.6B 2023 writedown.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **Cartel architecture effectively closed 2000-2001** via EU + US antitrust + Russia/Alrosa + Australia/Argyle entry — Sub-pattern B candidate (cartel architecture closed + niche persistence as De Beers brand). (2) **2024 Anglo American announcement** to spin off or sell De Beers — Sub-pattern A candidate (architecture restructured with potential successor entities). (3) **Lab-grown diamonds substrate shift catastrophic** — lab-grown 90% cheaper than mined (vs 10% in 2018); $4.5B synthetic sales cannibalized $7B+ natural diamond sales 2024-2025. (4) **De Beers ending Lightbox lab-grown brand 2024-2025** as part of spinoff preparation — strategic retreat from synthetic diamond extension. (5) Botswana + Angola September 2025 proposed majority stake purchase — voluntary restructuring with state participation. (6) **"A diamond is forever"** marketing remains canonical example of manufactured demand creation.
