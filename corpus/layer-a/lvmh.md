
# LVMH

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton SE formed June 1987 via merger of Moët Hennessy (1971 Moët + Hennessy merger) + Louis Vuitton (founded 1854). Bernard Arnault took control 1989 after hostile takeover; Chairman + CEO since. Five children of Arnault hold key executive positions across LVMH brands. 75+ brands across 6 segments: Fashion & Leather Goods (Louis Vuitton + Dior + Loro Piana + Fendi + Celine + Givenchy + Loewe + Marc Jacobs + others); Wines & Spirits (Moët + Hennessy + Dom Pérignon + Krug + others); Perfumes & Cosmetics (Guerlain + Givenchy + Christian Dior); Watches & Jewelry (Tiffany acquired 2021 $15.8B + Tag Heuer + Hublot + Zenith + Bvlgari); Selective Retailing (Sephora + DFS); Other Activities. FY2025 revenue €78.5B (down from ~€84B 2024) with operating profit €17.15B (down from €19.57B 2024). Market cap ~$370B (2026).

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **China slowdown 2023-2025 + recovery starting 2025-2026** — LVMH planning major Beijing flagships December 2025 + Milan + Tokyo flagships strong; aspirational luxury demand erosion + return to "aspirational" shoppers (Coach + Ralph Lauren competitive substitution). (2) Tiffany acquisition 2021 $15.8B major architectural extension into jewelry + American luxury. (3) Bernard Arnault age 77 + 5-child succession architecture explicit + decade-scale family stewardship pattern. (4) Aspirational luxury fatigue Western markets + Chinese luxury demand cyclical + recovery signals 2025-2026. (5) Hermès maintaining double-digit growth vs LVMH single-digit decline 2025 — competitive position erosion in high-luxury segment.
