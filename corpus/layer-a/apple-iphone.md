
# Apple iPhone + iOS

## Research Summary

Verified primary facts as of May 2026: Apple founded 1976 by Steve Jobs + Steve Wozniak + Ronald Wayne. iPhone launched January 9, 2007 at Macworld by Steve Jobs; on sale June 29, 2007. App Store launched July 10, 2008. Jobs CEO 1997-2011 (returned from NeXT acquisition); Tim Cook CEO since August 2011. Q1 FY2026 (ending Dec 27, 2025) revenue $143.8B (+16%); Q2 FY2026 (Mar 28, 2026) revenue $111.2B (+17%). iPhone 52% of quarterly revenue, Services 24% ($109B+ FY2025 first time crossing $100B), Wearables 9% ($35.6B), Mac 8%, iPad 7%. Apple smartphone share 20% global (+10% YoY 2025) — highest growth among top 5 vendors despite Android 71% global. ~$3.5T market cap.

Critical force-topology updates training data would miss: (1) **EU Digital Markets Act (DMA) effective March 7, 2024** — Apple designated gatekeeper; required to permit 3rd-party app stores on iOS in EU; Epic Games' competing game store now operating in EU after Apple initially blocked + DMA-compelled reversal. (2) **EU Commission June 2024 preliminary ruling** Apple in violation of anti-steering provisions; March 2025 official decision; potential fine up to 10% global revenue (~$38B). (3) **US DOJ March 2024 antitrust lawsuit** — most aggressive Big Tech antitrust action since 1990s; Ninth Circuit June 2025 denied Apple emergency stay; case returned to federal district court. (4) **Epic Games v. Apple US** — Apple appealing royalty-free compulsory IP license in Epic competition case; contempt ruling could lead to criminal prosecution. (5) **Apple Intelligence** AI integration launched 2024 + on-device processing via Neural Engine; iOS 26 + iPhone 17 Pro Max AI extension; competing with Galaxy AI + Pixel AI + Apple Intelligence. (6) Vision Pro mixed reality + spatial computing launched 2024 — Apple's strategic substrate extension attempt. (7) Apple Watch >50% smartwatch share since 2015 launch. (8) **iOS lock-in + ecosystem** = canonical identity + capability + ecosystem combined architecture per user observation.
